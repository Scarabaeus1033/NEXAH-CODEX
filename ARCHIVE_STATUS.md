# NEXAH-CODEX Archive Status

Status: `FROZEN HISTORICAL ARCHIVE`

NEXAH-CODEX records the Scarabæus exploratory phase that preceded the current
NEXAH orientation ecosystem. It is retained for developmental history,
inspection, citation, and reuse under the applicable licenses.

The archive is not:

- an active scientific research program;
- a source of current NEXAH architecture or governance authority;
- a claim that speculative or artistic material is established science; or
- a replacement for the current NEXAH repository.

Active framework development continues at:
https://github.com/Scarabaeus1031/NEXAH

The archive's scientific and historical wording is preserved. Governance,
licensing, provenance, security, or navigation clarifications may be added
without reopening theoretical expansion or silently rewriting earlier records.
