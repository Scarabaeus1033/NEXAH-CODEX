# NEXAH-CODEX Licensing Scope

NEXAH-CODEX is a frozen historical archive containing software, documentation,
research sketches, figures, artwork, datasets, media, quotations, and
source-derived material. Repository location alone does not establish
ownership or relicense a component.

## Current additional licenses

Effective for the repository state beginning with the licensing-alignment
commit, material for which Thomas K. R. Hofmann holds the required rights is
additionally offered as follows:

| Material | Additional license | Scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Original software, tests, configuration, and implementation code | [Apache 2.0](LICENSE-CODE.md) | Original archived code unless a file states otherwise |
| Original documentation, research text, figures, and artwork | [CC BY 4.0](LICENSE-DOCS.md) | Original authored material unless a file states otherwise |
| Original machine-readable datasets and derived numerical results published as data | [CC0 1.0](LICENSE-DATA.md) | Original data unless a file states otherwise; schemas and executable models follow their functional class |

## Historical license continuity

The repository and its releases were previously distributed under
[CC BY-NC-SA 4.0](LICENSE-LEGACY.md). Those grants remain valid and are not
revoked. In particular, the Zenodo release `v1.0.1` retains its recorded
CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 license and citation metadata.

Recipients may rely on an applicable license offered for the exact material
and version they received. This additional licensing statement does not claim
to alter copies, releases, or components controlled by another rights holder.

## Exclusions

Third-party material, quotations, source-derived artifacts, embedded media,
fonts, libraries, and files carrying local terms remain subject to their stated
source licenses and provenance. If no reuse right is recorded, repository
access alone does not grant permission to reuse the material.

Moving, indexing, rendering, or referencing an artifact does not change its
license. Licensing also does not promote an exploratory statement into an
established scientific claim or make the archive an active NEXAH authority.
