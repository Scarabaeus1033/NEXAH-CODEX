# NEXAH-CODEX Licensing Index

NEXAH-CODEX is a frozen, mixed-material archive. Licensing follows material
type, provenance, and release history rather than one undifferentiated license.

The controlling scope is documented in [LICENSES.md](LICENSES.md).

- Original archived software: [Apache License 2.0](LICENSE-CODE.md)
- Original documentation, research text, figures, and artwork:
  [CC BY 4.0](LICENSE-DOCS.md)
- Original machine-readable datasets and derived numerical results published
  as data: [CC0 1.0](LICENSE-DATA.md)
- Historical releases and prior distributions: their original
  [CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 terms](LICENSE-LEGACY.md) remain valid
- Third-party and source-derived material: its stated source terms; not
  relicensed by NEXAH-CODEX

See [ARCHIVE_STATUS.md](ARCHIVE_STATUS.md), [NOTICE](NOTICE), and
[TRADEMARKS.md](TRADEMARKS.md) for archive, origin, and naming boundaries.
